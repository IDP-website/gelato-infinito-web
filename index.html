From farm to scoop

Simple ingredients. Infinite flavour.

Every scoop starts with a plant, a fruit or a farm. We use the freshest local milk and cream, real fruit, real nuts and real vanilla bean, then churn it slowly the Italian way so it comes out dense, silky and full of flavour.

  • Made by hand, dailySmall batches, churned on site.
  • 100% local dairyFresh milk and cream from Leeuwenbosch Dairy.
  • Dairy-free sorbettoBright, real-fruit sorbets for a clean, cool scoop.
